Privacy Policy

Resmara (Real Estate Boost Engine) — last updated 2026

The short version. Resmara turns a property listing into a marketing campaign for you, the agent who runs it. We store the account and property details you give us, we ask the advertising platforms you connect (Meta) to run the campaign you approved, and we never sell your data or post anything you have not approved.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name, and a password hash (we never store the password itself).
  • Listing content: the property address, facts and photos you submit, plus the captions, hashtags and creative we generate from them.
  • Connected-platform data: when you connect Meta, we receive an access token plus the ad accounts, Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts you authorise. The token is stored server-side and used only to publish and run the campaigns you approve.
  • Service records: campaign state, support tickets you open, and error information used to operate the service.

How we use it

  • To generate the campaign drafts you review and approve.
  • To create and (only when you unpause it) run the advertising campaign on your ad account.
  • To publish the posts you approved to the Page or Instagram account you selected.
  • To answer support requests and keep the service running.

What we send to third parties

  • Meta Platforms (facebook.com) receives the campaign, ad creative and post content you approved, so it can be published and advertised under your account.
  • Model providers receive the property facts and listing photos needed to draft the copy and images. They are used to produce drafts, not to train your data into public models.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never post to your Page or Instagram account, and never start ad spend, without your approval. Campaigns are created paused; only you unpause them.
  • We never store your Facebook password, and we do not hold your card details.

Retention and deletion

Account and campaign records are kept while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account, your listings and your stored platform tokens at any time by emailing the address below or opening a support ticket in the app; we complete deletion within 30 days. Uploaded listing photos and generated creatives are removed with them.

Security

Access tokens and passwords are stored server-side, never in the browser. Traffic is served over HTTPS. Any leaked or expired platform token causes the connection to be rejected rather than reused.

Children

Resmara is a business tool for licensed real-estate professionals and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions, or want your data removed? Email support@trackmygold.net or use the Support page inside the app.
